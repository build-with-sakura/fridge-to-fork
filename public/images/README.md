## Photo rules

- Format: WebP.
- Size: 1200 x 800 pixels, landscape.
- File size: aim under 150 KB. Maximum 300 KB.
- Keep the dish in the centre, with space around it. The app crops each photo to fit different card shapes.
- Style: photorealistic, bright natural daylight, clean light background.
- Use only photos you own or have the right to use.
- Add a credit to `imageCredit` in `src/data/recipes.js` only if the photo source requires one.

Until a photo is added, the app shows the drawn dish instead.
